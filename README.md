### LAB 9 – Marketing Copy and Compliance

In Lab 9, I learned how to create aviation marketing content for different digital platforms while maintaining advertising compliance. The campaign focused on SkyLine Airways' Premium Economy Flex Class, which included features such as 20% extra legroom, priority check-in and boarding, complimentary hot meals, and flexible ticket changes.

The activity required creating different marketing formats including a LinkedIn post, Instagram caption, advertisement headline, and promotional email. I learned that marketing messages must remain factually accurate and should not contain unsupported claims.

The lab also examined misleading statements such as claiming to offer the cheapest tickets in India or guaranteed zero delays. Such claims were identified as non-compliant because they were unsupported or unrealistic. The revised advertisement used factual product benefits and included appropriate terms and conditions. This activity taught me that effective marketing should be attractive while remaining accurate, transparent, and compliant with advertising requirements.
